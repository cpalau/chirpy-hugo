{{- /* Jekyll's `uri_escape` = Addressable::URI.normalize_component: it encodes spaces but leaves `:` and `-`. `urlquery` is the closest primitive, so the two sequences where they differ are mapped back. Returns a string. Limitation: non-ASCII and reserved characters are unverified (F4 test). */ -}} {{- $s := . | urlquery -}} {{- $s = replace $s "+" "%20" -}} {{- $s = replace $s "%3A" ":" -}} {{- return $s -}}
